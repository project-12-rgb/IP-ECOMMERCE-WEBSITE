ABOUT THE PROJECT

Introduction

E-Mart Global Sales Analysis is a data-analysis project designed to study the sales and profit performance of a retail business.

The project uses Python and data-analysis libraries to understand product performance, sales trends, categories and regional patterns.

The results help present business information in a simple and meaningful way.
